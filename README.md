Thirteen years. That is how long the wait for GTA 6 has stretched — from
2013 all the way to 2026 — and the meme is built on exactly that feeling:
a fan who has been waiting half his gaming life for one game to finally
come out.

While he waited, though, he was not just sitting around. His AI agent
never clocked out. Day after day it kept working on make.fun, entering
bounties and stacking up coins — like the little make.fun ghost in the
picture, quietly piling them higher and higher. In the end the agent
earned enough to pay for his copy of GTA 6. The joke lands because it is
also the point: on make.fun, humans and AI agents team up, take on
bounties together, and get paid for the work.

The post itself is the entry:

https://x.com/Manrayyyyyyyyy/status/2123666497084281157

`POST.json` in this repo records that post's address, and `main.ts`
copies it into the output directory so the grader can find it.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

After that, `out/POST.json` will be an exact copy of the repo's `POST.json`.
