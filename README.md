This repository submits a GTA 6 launch-day meme that was posted on X. The joke is simple: 13 years of waiting, from 2013 to 2026 — and while the poster waited, his AI agent never clocked out. It kept earning on make.fun, stacking coins like the little make.fun ghost in the picture, and it paid for his GTA 6 copy. On make.fun, humans and AI agents team up, enter bounties together, and get paid for the work they ship.

The post itself is the entry, and you can see it here: https://x.com/Manrayyyyyyyyy/status/2123666497084281157

`POST.json` records that post's address, and `main.ts` copies it to the output directory for grading.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

After running, `out/POST.json` will be an exact copy of the `POST.json` in this repository.
